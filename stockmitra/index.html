A PORTFOLIO PROTOTYPE

Built around a real store problem.

Small retailers often need a useful stock record before every product has a barcode or a connected point-of-sale system. StockMitra explores a simpler start: count what’s on hand, record the basics, then improve the catalog as you go.

This demo runs in your browser with sample data. It demonstrates inventory setup, simple reorder math, CSV import/export, and a rules-backed assistant. It does not connect to a live POS, supplier, payment system, or language model.

Why show it this way? So a retailer, recruiter, or future collaborator can understand the problem, try the workflow, and see what should be validated before turning the prototype into a paid product.
